## Maintainer decision — 2026-10-18

The maintainer chose option A in the decision interview: support write-only battery clients and return a minimal receipt.

- Tracking a charge requires BATTERIES_TRACK_CHARGE_CYCLE; it does not additionally require BATTERIES_VIEW.
- A caller without BATTERIES_VIEW receives success and the new charge-cycle ID for undo, without the full battery_charge_cycles row.
- Full row data requires BATTERIES_VIEW.
- Undo retains its separate BATTERIES_UNDO_CHARGE_CYCLE permission and empty success response; it does not additionally require BATTERIES_VIEW.

The maintainer's rationale is that smart charging stations exist and could report battery charging through an integration. No smart charging station is available to the maintainer for hardware testing.

Implementation must update the documented response contract and add regression tests for track-only and undo-only callers, including the minimal receipt and the full response with view access. The broader existence and deletion findings remain tracked in #909.

This records a policy decision. Implementation and regression verification remain outstanding; keep this issue open.
